General Rules
- Total team members: 3
- Only 2 members from each team will participate.
LEVEL 1 — ACTION LA ANSWER
- The host will provide two movie titles to each team.
- One participant must act out one movie title.
- The other participant must guess the movie.
- All teams will participate simultaneously.
Points:
- 10 points for finding the answer within 10 seconds.
- 5 points for finding the answer within 20 seconds.
LEVEL 2 — TUNE-UH PAATHU SOLLU
- The host will provide a random word and a song name to each team.
- One participant must give a tune using the given word.
- The other participant must identify the song.
Points:
- 10 points for finding the answer within 5 seconds.
- 5 points for finding the answer within 10 seconds.
- The two teams with the highest scores will qualify for Level 3.
LEVEL 3 — FINAL ROUND
- The host will play a song and stop it midway.
- Participants from the two finalist teams must identify and sing the missing lyrics.
- The participant who picks up the microphone first gets the chance to answer.
- If the answer is incorrect or the participant does not know the lyrics, the opposing team gets the chance to answer.
- The team that successfully completes the final challenge will be declared the Winner.

1. FLIP TAC TOE
Rules & Regulations
1. On the signal, the player must flip the cup and make it land upside down.
2. If the cup falls or fails to land correctly, the player must retry until successful.
3. After successfully flipping the cup, the player must place it on any empty square of the Tic-Tac-Toe board.
4. The turn passes to the next player after the cup is successfully placed.
5. The first player or team to get three cups in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the round.
---
2. WALKER BOTTLE
Rules & Regulations
1. A straight ladder-shaped grid with an odd number of squares will be marked on the playing surface.
2. The middle square will be marked with an "X", and a marker bottle will be placed on it.
3. Each player will be provided with a water bottle filled approximately two-thirds full.
4. On the whistle, players must repeatedly flip the bottle and make it land upright.
5. For every successful bottle flip, the player can move the marker bottle one space towards the opponent's side.
6. The first player to move the marker bottle completely to the opponent's side wins.
7. If the time limit expires, the player whose marker is closest to the opponent's side wins.
---
3. BALLOON CUP
Rules & Regulations
1. Players are not allowed to touch the cups directly with their hands.
2. An uninflated balloon must be placed inside a plastic cup.
3. The balloon must be inflated sufficiently to grip the inner surface of the cup.
4. Players must lift and move the cup using only the balloon.
5. The cup must be placed on another cup to build the required structure.
6. The balloon must be deflated gently to release the cup.
7. The first player to complete the required stack wins.
---
4. BALLOON PYRAMID
Rules & Regulations
1. Players are not allowed to touch the cups directly with their hands.
2. A deflated balloon must be inserted inside a cup and inflated to grip the cup.
3. Players must transport the cup using only the balloon.
4. The cups must be placed layer by layer to construct the required pyramid.
5. If a cup falls or the pyramid collapses, the player must continue using the balloon technique to reposition the cup.
6. The first player or team to successfully complete the pyramid wins.
---
5. BOTTLE BOMB
Rules & Regulations
1. Players must complete the given challenge within the allotted time.
2. The challenge may include bottle flipping, matching, or other tasks specified by the event coordinator.
3. One player will begin the challenge when instructed by the coordinator.
4. After successfully completing the challenge, the player must pass the turn to the next participant.
5. If the timer expires before the challenge is completed, the player loses that round.
6. The player or team with the highest number of successful rounds or points will be declared the winner.
